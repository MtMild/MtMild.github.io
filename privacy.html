Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

This tool is used only by the owner of the channels Cents Later, Everyday Mind and Tiny Tune Town, on the owner's own Google accounts. It has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, it accesses those channels through YouTube API Services: their videos, metadata, comments and statistics. Google sign-in tokens are stored only in the owner's private storage.

What it does not do

It does not collect data about viewers or other people beyond what YouTube returns for the owner's own channels, such as public comments and aggregate statistics. It does not sell or share this data and does not use it for advertising.

Google and YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services, subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be withdrawn at any time in Google account permissions.
